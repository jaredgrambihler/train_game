How to ride

You get four digits. Keep them in the order shown and add operators to make exactly 10.

2 2 4 2 → 2 + 2 + 4 + 2 or 2 × 2 + 4 + 2
1 4 2 9 → 1 % 42 + 9

+ − × ÷add, subtract, multiply, divide
//floor divide: 7 // 2 = 3
%remainder: 7 % 3 = 1
xypower, typed ^ or **: 2^3 = 8
42tap digits back to back to join them into one number (it can’t start with 0)
( ) and −parentheses and a minus sign can go anywhere

Math follows Python’s rules. Powers come first and stack right to left, so 2^3^2 is 2^9. A leading minus applies after the power, so −2^2 is −4; write (−2)^2 for 4.

Every puzzle has at least one answer. Each one you solve moves the train a stop down the line. Show route reveals an answer but resets your streak.

On a keyboard: type digits and operators, Enter to check, Backspace to delete, Esc to clear.
